About us

Built on jobs done right the first time

CT Cleaning Service started with a simple standard: show up, do the work properly, and leave the client able to trust us with the next one.

Our story

What began as cleaning work for individual homes and church congregations around Nairobi — including PCEA churches — has grown into a business trusted with larger institutional contracts, including work for campuses like Northlands.

That growth happened one job at a time, by treating a small house the same way we'd treat a large facility: with a clear scope, a reliable crew, and a walkthrough before we call it done.

What we stand for

A few principles that shape every job, regardless of size.

Reliability

We show up when we say we will, and we finish what we start.

Trained crews

Every team member knows the standard we hold jobs to — not just "clean," but done properly.

Clear communication

You know the scope, the schedule, and who to contact before work starts.

Accountability

If something's missed, we hear about it and fix it — no excuses.
