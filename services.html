Services

One standard of clean, scaled to any job

Whether it's a single house or a full institutional campus, every job follows the same process: a clear scope, trained crews, and a walkthrough before we sign off.

Institutional & large-scale cleaning

For schools, campuses, and large facilities like Northlands, we run coordinated multi-person crews on a fixed schedule, with a named point of contact and reporting your facilities team can rely on.

  • Scheduled contract cleaning (daily/weekly)
  • Multi-crew coordination for large floor areas
  • Post-event and post-construction cleanups
  • Reporting and a dedicated point of contact

Office cleaning

Daily, weekly, or on-demand cleaning that keeps a workplace presentable — desks, common areas, washrooms, and kitchens — without disrupting your team's day.

  • Flexible scheduling around working hours
  • Washroom and kitchen sanitation
  • Waste management and restocking supplies
  • One-off deep cleans before client visits or audits

Churches & events

Pre- and post-service cleaning for congregations, including PCEA churches, plus one-off turnarounds for weddings, conferences, and other events.

  • Weekly or bi-weekly church cleaning arrangements
  • Fast turnaround after events
  • Hall, sanctuary, and washroom cleaning
  • Flexible one-off bookings for special occasions

Home cleaning

Deep cleaning, move-in/move-out cleaning, and regular household cleaning for homes across Nairobi.

  • One-off deep cleans
  • Move-in / move-out cleaning
  • Recurring weekly or monthly visits
  • Kitchen, bathroom, and full-house options
